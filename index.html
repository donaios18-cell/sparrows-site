🔔 Reminders

It keeps asking, so you don't have to

A task with a deadline is not a note that quietly expires. Sparrows nudges the person it was given to — every 30 minutes, every hour, whatever you chose — until the job is marked done.

  • Repeats stop the moment the task is finished
  • Quiet hours per person: nobody gets buzzed at 2 a.m.
  • Children can set reminders for parents too — and parents can cap how often
  • Anyone can mute a single task without muting the app

📸 Proof and review

“I did it” — show me

Turn on the check and a finished task does not close on its own: it comes to you with a photo. Accept it, or send it back with one line about what is still wrong.

🎁 Points and rewards

Pocket money, film night, one hour of anything

Every task can be worth points. Points pile up, and the family decides what they buy. A child asks for a reward, a parent approves it — and the balance does the arguing instead of you.

  • Rewards are yours to invent: screen time, a pizza Friday, a trip to the cinema
  • Parents can add or take away points by hand, with a reason attached
  • Everyone sees where their points came from

🔒 Private by default

A task is between the two of you

Every task is visible to the person who gave it and the person who has to do it — nobody else, not even the other parent. If you want it shared, there is a switch for that, and children have it too.

  • The rule lives in the database, not in the app's screens
  • A teenager can give a parent a task without the whole family reading it
  • No sign-up means no email list, no password to leak
  • Lost the phone? Another parent hands your profile back, or Apple ID does it for you

Questions

Short answers

Do the children need their own Apple ID?

No. A child opens the app, types the invite code and their name, and they are in. No email, no password, nothing to remember.

Can children give tasks to parents?

Yes, and they usually do within the first hour. Parents can decide how often a child's reminders may repeat — “as they set them”, “no more than every two hours”, or “no repeats”.

What happens when a phone is lost?

The profile stays with the family, not with the phone. Another parent can hand it back to a new phone with all tasks and points. A parent who linked their Apple ID can also restore it alone.

Does everyone see every task?

No. A task is private between the author and the people it is for. Sharing it with the other parents is a switch, off by default — and children have that switch too.

Will it nag at night?

No. Everyone sets their own quiet hours, and repeat reminders wait until those are over.

Is one purchase really enough for the whole family?

Yes. Whoever buys it unlocks the family; everybody else just keeps using the app. There is nothing to renew and nothing to cancel.
