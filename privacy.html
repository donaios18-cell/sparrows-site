Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

The short version

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is ever sold or shared for marketing.
  • No email address, no password, no phone number, no contacts, no location.
  • What we do keep is what the app needs to work: the names you type, the tasks you give each other, the photos you attach, points and rewards.
  • Leave the family and your data is deleted. When the last person leaves, the family and everything in it goes with them.

Sparrows (“the app”) is made by NG Hive, an independent studio run by Natalia Gurevich in the United States. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, where, who can see it and how to get rid of it. Questions: contact@nghive.app.

There is no sign-up

Sparrows has no accounts in the usual sense. You never give us an email address or a password. When the app first opens, it creates an anonymous identity for that phone on our server, and the key to it is kept in the iOS Keychain on your device. Inside the family you are a member — a name and an avatar you choose yourself.

You may optionally link Sign in with Apple to protect your profile. If you do, we receive only Apple's stable identifier for your Apple ID — not your name, not your email address, not anything else. It exists so that you can get your own profile back on a new phone.

What we never collect

  • No advertising identifiers, no advertising networks, no ad SDKs — there are no ads in the app.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK. We do not know how often you open the app or what you tap.
  • No location, no contacts, no calendar, no microphone.
  • No email addresses, passwords or phone numbers.
  • Camera and photo access are asked for only when you attach a photo to a task, and only the photo you pick is uploaded.

On your phone

The key to your anonymous identity lives in the iOS Keychain. The app also keeps a small technical log on the device to help with support; it stays on your phone unless you decide to send it to us.

Notifications

A notification contains the name of the person who gave or finished the task and the task's title — the same text you would see inside the app. Since it can appear on a lock screen, keep that in mind when naming a delicate task.

Notifications are delivered through Expo's push service and then Apple's Push Notification service. Undelivered items are removed from our queue after seven days.

Who can see what

  • A task is private by default. Only the person who gave it and the people it was given to can see it — not the other parents, unless the author turns on sharing. Children have that switch too.
  • These rules are enforced by the database itself, not just hidden in the interface.
  • Everyone in the family sees the family's members, their names, avatars and point balances, and the rewards list.
  • Nobody outside your family can see any of it. We do not read your tasks, and we have no interest in them.

Children

Sparrows is built for families and is meant to be set up by a parent, who then invites the children. A child's record holds only the name and avatar chosen for them, their tasks, points and any photos they attach. There is no profiling, no advertising and no marketing directed at anyone, children included, and children cannot be contacted through the app by anyone outside their family.

A parent can remove a child from the family at any moment; that deletes the child's tasks, points and photos. If you believe a child's data reached us in a way you did not intend, write to contact@nghive.app and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

  • Leave the family (Settings → Leave the family) and your member record, your tasks and your points are deleted.
  • A parent removing a member deletes that person's data the same way.
  • When the last member leaves, the family itself is deleted along with its tasks, photos, rewards and history.
  • Or simply write to us and we will delete the family for you.

We keep the record of a purchase after that, because Apple ties it to your Apple ID and it is what lets you restore the purchase later.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase — database, private file storage and the server functions, in the United States.
  • Expo — relays push notifications to Apple.
  • Apple — delivers notifications, handles purchases, and provides Sign in with Apple if you use it.

That is the entire list. There are no advertising or analytics companies in it.

Your rights

Ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, or ask us to delete it: contact@nghive.app. Since we hold no email address, please write from an address we can answer, and tell us the family name and your name in the app so we can find the right records.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If a change matters, we will say so in the app.
